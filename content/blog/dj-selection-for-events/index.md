## Evaluating DJs after receiving demos

Let’s assume a scenario: I’m coordinating or counselling for building a DJ lineup for an event. I have received a bunch of demos where some are from DJays interested in or attending the event and some others are recommendations made to me for booking.

How do I filter and select who to book for the event? I first consider the event itself then start to look at each individual DJays.
